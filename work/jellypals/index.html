THE OVERVIEW

A conceptual interactive platform inspired by the emotional companionship associated with plush toys. Jellypals explores character interaction, community features, augmented reality experiences and virtual spaces through a mobile interface concept. It is an independent academic exploration of digital companionship, rather than an official Jellycat product or a launched commercial application.

The objective

Explore how the comfort and character of physical plush toys might translate into an interactive digital experience.

The approach

Bring character interaction, community and virtual spaces into a proposed mobile experience. Interface concepts explore these ideas visually; augmented reality and community features are proposals rather than implemented services.
