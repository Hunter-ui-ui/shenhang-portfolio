PROJECT TYPE

University / Exhibition & Digital Experience Proposal

THE OVERVIEW

An exhibition planning proposal covering eight zones, themed visitor routes, interactive experiences and marketing concepts. The project also included an initial concept for a robot competition prediction and results-tracking platform with real-time data visualisation. The proposal was adopted for further consideration and development; proposed digital features were not a completed public product.

The objective

Connect exhibition planning, visitor engagement and digital interaction within a coherent event proposal.

The approach

Structure the experience around eight exhibition zones and themed visitor routes, with supporting campaign concepts. Explore a prediction and results-tracking interface as a potential digital extension of the robot competition.
